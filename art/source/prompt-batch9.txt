Same game, same chat. Every earlier FLICK image here is the STYLE AND PALETTE reference, and batch 8 (the department bosses and their barricades) is the closest match for this batch. Match it exactly: clean flat 2D vector cartoon, bold even dark-brown outline #332A24 (about 12px on a 1024px canvas), flat warm muted fills, one cel-shade shape and one small flat highlight per shape, light from the upper left. The departments surrendered, but their people have dug in at the floor's last two shared spaces, the Break Room and the Corner Conference Room, behind walls of filing cabinets. This batch is the two people holding them (four faces each), their filing-cabinet walls (standing, and broken through), and two small icons.

Rules for EVERY image: transparent PNG (RGBA); no background, floor, cast shadow or border; no gradients, painterly texture, noise, photorealism or 3D rendering; ABSOLUTELY NO letters, numbers, words, logos, brand marks, cartoon characters or printed graphics anywhere, including on clothing, mugs, cabinets, drawer labels (leave label slots blank), whiteboards, flip charts and screens (the game draws all text itself). Scripted finishing (exact canvas size, clean alpha, alignment, matching pairs) is authorized as before.

Number the files exactly as below (they continue after 196_white_flag.png). Deliver all 14 PNGs in a folder named batch9 inside the outputs folder, plus a zip of that folder, plus asset-manifest-batch9.json (size, alpha bounding box and the registration numbers asked for below, per file) and prompts-batch9.json (the prompt used for each file), like earlier batches.

FORMAT for all four: EXACTLY the batch 8 barricade format: 1400 x 800 transparent PNG, seen STRAIGHT ON at eye level from across the office, drawn at the SAME SCALE as the portraits (a head is 360px wide ear to ear; a four-drawer filing cabinet is about 300px wide and 860px tall, so two-drawer cabinets are about 430px tall), resting on an invisible desk-top line at y=780, horizontally centred on x=700, between about 1100 and 1250px wide.

Each room has a STANDING and a BROKEN version on the SAME canvas: the two END TOWERS (the outer 250px or so on each side) are pixel-identical in both, so the game can swap them in place. Only the middle changes.
  STANDING: the middle band (x=500 to 900) is a solid wall whose top edge sits between y=300 and y=340.
  BROKEN: the middle band (x=500 to 900) is no higher than y=520: cabinets knocked over, dented and tipped on their sides, drawers hanging open and loose paper spilling out.
Record the desk line (780), the scale, each wall's alpha bounding box, and the topmost alpha y inside x=500..900 in the manifest.
