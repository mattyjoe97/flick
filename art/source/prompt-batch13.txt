FLICK — ART BATCH 13: Chad, the golden stapler, office chaos and the Challenges app

Same game, same chat. Every earlier FLICK image here is the STYLE AND PALETTE reference for this batch: clean flat 2D vector cartoon, bold even dark-brown outline #332A24, flat warm muted fills, one cel-shade shape and one small flat highlight per shape, light from the upper left. Adult proportions, never chibi.

Four new things came into the game: a rival named Chad who races the player at every new job, a golden stapler that turns up for a few seconds and gives a bonus when tapped, office-wide chaos events (a fire drill, a power outage, a birthday cake, a surprise inspection), and a daily Challenges app on the player's computer. This batch is Chad's portrait (three faces), the golden stapler, four small chaos icons and one desktop app icon.

Rules for EVERY image: transparent PNG (RGBA); no background, floor, cast shadow or border; no gradients, painterly texture, noise, photorealism or 3D rendering; ABSOLUTELY NO letters, numbers, words, logos, brand marks, cartoon characters or printed graphics anywhere, including on clothing, badges, the cake, the clipboard and the icons (the game draws all text itself). Scripted finishing (exact canvas size, clean alpha, alignment, matching faces) is authorized as before.

Number the files exactly as below (they continue after batch 12's 224_view_floor30.png). Deliver all 9 PNGs in a folder named batch13 inside the outputs folder, plus a zip of that folder, plus asset-manifest-batch13.json (size and alpha bounding box per file) and prompts-batch13.json (the prompt used for each file), like earlier batches.
