FLICK — ART BATCH 8: the Department War (Stage 3)

Same game, same chat. Every earlier FLICK image here (the office scene, the desk props, Dale and his closet, the painted coworker portraits from batches 4, 6 and 7, the rubber band ball) is the STYLE AND PALETTE reference for this batch. Match it exactly: clean flat 2D vector cartoon, bold even dark-brown outline #332A24 (about 12px on a 1024px canvas), flat warm muted fills, one cel-shade shape and one small flat highlight per shape, light from the upper left. After beating Rick, the player takes the war to the rest of the floor. Four departments each have a boss who fights from behind a barricade made of their department's junk. This batch is those four bosses (four faces each), their four barricades, and a surrender flag.

Rules for EVERY image: transparent PNG (RGBA); no background, floor, cast shadow or border; no gradients, painterly texture, noise, photorealism or 3D rendering; ABSOLUTELY NO letters, numbers, words, logos, brand marks, cartoon characters or printed graphics anywhere, including on clothing, screens, binder spines, paper, stickers and badges (the game draws all text itself). Scripted finishing (exact canvas size, clean alpha, alignment, matching pairs) is authorized as before.

Number the files exactly as below (they continue after 175_id_badge.png). Deliver all 21 PNGs in a folder named batch8 inside the outputs folder, plus a zip of that folder, plus asset-manifest-batch8.json (size, alpha bounding box and the registration numbers asked for below, per file) and prompts-batch8.json (the prompt used for each file), like earlier batches.
