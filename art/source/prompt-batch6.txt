FLICK — ART BATCH 6: the work computer, the lackeys and the rubber band ball

Same game, same chat. Every earlier FLICK image here (the office scene, the desk drawer and props, Dale and his closet, the painted coworker portraits, the batch 5 desk, icons, coin and cash) is the STYLE AND PALETTE reference for this batch. Match it exactly: clean flat 2D vector cartoon, bold even dark-brown outline #332A24 (about 12px on a 1024px canvas, scaled down proportionally on smaller canvases), flat warm muted fills, one cel-shade shape and one small flat highlight per shape, light from the upper left. Adult proportions, never chibi.

Rules for EVERY image unless an item says otherwise: transparent PNG (RGBA); no background, floor, cast shadow or border; no gradients, painterly texture, noise, photorealism or 3D rendering; ABSOLUTELY NO letters, numbers, words, logos, brand marks or watermarks anywhere (the game draws all text itself). Scripted finishing (exact canvas size, clean alpha, alignment, matching pairs) is authorized as before.

Number the files exactly as below (they continue after 108_cash.png). Deliver all 42 PNGs in a folder named batch6 inside the outputs folder, plus a zip of that folder, plus asset-manifest-batch6.json (size, alpha bounding box and the registration numbers asked for below, per file) and prompts-batch6.json (the prompt used for each file), like earlier batches.
